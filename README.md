# hey, I'm Shrey 👋

I'm a Computer Engineering student at **McGill University**, with a minor in **Applied AI**. I like building things I can actually use: apps that make a busy day easier, AI tools that take repetitive work off someone's plate, and games people want to come back to.

My projects tend to cross a few boundaries. One day it's a React Native screen or a Python API; another it's a Roblox gameplay system, a Blender asset, or sensor data from a race car. I enjoy connecting those pieces and getting the whole thing working.

## Right now

- 🌱 Building **Velrey Development**, the software company I founded, and its productivity apps: [LifeLayer](https://github.com/shreysprojects/lifelayer), [TimeLog](https://github.com/shreysprojects/TimeLog), and [HabitLog](https://github.com/shreysprojects/HabitLog). They're designed with ADHD in mind, and I'm using them myself as I work toward an App Store launch.
- 🏎️ Working on **LiDAR perception with McGill Formula Electric's driverless subteam**, helping the car detect obstacles and understand its surroundings.
- 🛠️ Building out projects across **applied AI, mobile apps, and multiplayer games**, and sharing more of the work here.

## A few things I've built

- **AI tools at AI-Intelekt:** during my summer 2026 internship, I built service-record analysis, translation and writing-correction APIs, Jira automation, and a [content pipeline](https://github.com/shreysprojects/ai-intelekt-engine) with fact-checking and human approval. My [service-desk AI work](https://github.com/shreysprojects/intellect-projects) also explores local models, structured outputs, and layered translation checks on CPU-only hardware.
- **Productivity apps:** [LifeLayer's app code](https://github.com/shreysprojects/ProductivityNative-) brings together routines, fitness, nutrition, cloud sync, and AI features. TimeLog and HabitLog focus on the smaller daily systems around it.
- **Games and 3D tools:** shipped Roblox multiplayer games reaching roughly **1,000 concurrent players**, and led core scripting on a team that won **1st place at a McGill GameDev Club game jam**. I've also built [Unity projects](https://github.com/shreysprojects/Rollaball) and a [Blender-to-Roblox asset pipeline](https://github.com/shreysprojects/BlenderExports).
- **Engineering and automation:** built an Arduino-based, 3D-printed obstacle-avoiding car; converted MATLAB workflows to Maple at Metallurgical Sensors; and made tools for browser automation, CSV processing, and reproducible reports.

I started building real estate websites during the pandemic. Since then, the projects have changed, but I still like taking an idea through the design, code, testing, and all the small fixes that make it useful.
